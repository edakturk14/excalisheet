# Excalisheet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Excalisheet is a skills-only ChatGPT plugin that turns technical content into editable Excalidraw diagrams and cheat sheets.

## What Excalisheet collects

Excalisheet does not operate its own external server, database, analytics service, or user account system.

Excalisheet does not independently collect, store, sell, or share your prompts, documents, URLs, generated diagrams, or personal information.

## How your content is used

When you use Excalisheet in ChatGPT, the content you provide is processed inside ChatGPT so the plugin can create the requested visual.

If you provide a public webpage or documentation URL, ChatGPT may access that page in order to understand the content and create the visual.

Excalisheet itself does not retain a separate copy of that content.

## OpenAI

Your use of ChatGPT is also subject to OpenAI's own terms and privacy practices. Excalisheet does not control how the ChatGPT platform handles data.

## Third-party content

If you provide content or URLs from another website, you are responsible for making sure you have the right to use that material.

## Changes

If Excalisheet later adds an external server, account system, analytics, or other data collection, this policy will be updated before that functionality is released publicly.

## Contact

For questions or privacy concerns, open an issue in the Excalisheet GitHub repository:
